## About the Activity

For Activity 4, our group worked on a presentation involving Google Docs. This activity gave us an opportunity to explore how digital tools can help us create, organize, and share information while working with other group members.

## What I Learned

This activity helped me understand the importance of digital collaboration and using online tools for academic work.

I learned how Google Docs can make group tasks more convenient because members can contribute, make revisions, and share their work in one place.

One challenge in group activities is keeping everyone's contributions organized and consistent. Working with a shared document can help address this by allowing members to review changes and coordinate their work.

In the future, I can apply these skills when preparing nursing reports, research papers, group presentations, and other academic requirements.

## Skills Developed

- Digital collaboration
- Document formatting
- Online communication
- Teamwork and organization
