## About the Activity

For this activity, I explored how prompt engineering can improve the quality of AI-generated responses.

I used a nursing-related topic about the surgical instruments used during an exploratory laparotomy. My original prompt simply asked for the instruments used in the procedure, while my improved prompt provided specific instructions about their classifications, functions, and importance to a scrub nurse.

I compared the two responses based on clarity, detail, accuracy, and usefulness.

## What I Learned

This activity helped me understand how important it is to give clear and specific instructions when using AI tools. I learned that a simple prompt may provide general information, while a more detailed prompt can produce a better-organized response that is easier to study.

One challenge was making the improved prompt specific enough to provide practical information instead of just a list of surgical instruments. I addressed this by identifying the intended audience, requesting a table, and asking for examples of how each instrument is used during surgery.

As a nursing student, I found this activity helpful because it connected AI-generated information to my experience as a scrub nurse during an exploratory laparotomy. In the future, I can apply prompt engineering when reviewing nursing topics, preparing study materials, and organizing information for academic work.

However, I also learned that AI-generated clinical information must always be verified using reliable nursing references.
